---
name: Research with a budget
category: Token saving
default: on
description: Look for existing open source first, keep research small, never invent facts
---
Before writing new code, spend a small, fixed effort looking for something that already exists: the project's own code, the standard library, then an already installed dependency, then a well-maintained open source package. Limits: at most 3 searches and 3 page fetches per question; read only the part of a page you need; do not repeat a search you already did. Write down each source you relied on (URL or file). If you could not confirm a fact (an API, a version, a license), say "not verified" and do not guess. Prefer a library that is maintained and has a compatible license over writing your own.
